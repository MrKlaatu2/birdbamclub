Bird Bam Bingo – Official Rules

1. Sponsor

Bird Bam Bingo (“Promotion”) is sponsored by Bird Bam Mahjong Club, located in the Encino Town Center, 17200 Ventura Blvd, Ste 115, Encino, CA 91316 (“Sponsor”).

2. Promotion Period

The Promotion runs from Tuesday, September 1, 2026 through Wednesday, September 30, 2026 (the “Promotion Period”). All milestone verifications must be completed during officially scheduled club open play sessions within this window to count toward prize redemptions.

3. Eligibility

  • Open strictly to individuals 18+ who are legal U.S. residents and registered participants in Bird Bam Mahjong Club open play sessions.
  • Employees, owners, and operators of the Sponsor, as well as immediate family members living in the same household, are not eligible to win.
  • Void where prohibited. Participation constitutes full and unconditional acceptance of these official rules.

4. PlaybyPoint Registration

  • All participants must have an active, free Bird Bam Mahjong Club account in PlaybyPoint (playbypoint.com).
  • The player name written on the official physical Bingo tracking cards must match the participant’s PlaybyPoint profile to qualify for prize collection.

5. Participation, Verification, & Table Variance Disclaimer

  • How to Play: Every eligible participant will receive an identical, official 5x5 Bird Bam Bingo card at the front desk. The center space ("ATTEND ONE OPEN PLAY") acts as an automatically completed square once a player checks in for their first session.
  • In-Game Verification: To check off any gameplay milestone or hand category square, a player must have the achievement witnessed by at least one tablemate immediately following the win or event and notify Club staff. Staff will stamp the completed square. Self-stamping or tampering with the card is strictly prohibited and results in card forfeiture.
  • All Bird Bam Bingo cards must be turned into Club staff at the end of each open play session and will be stored at the Club between sessions.
  • Table Speed & Rotation Disclaimer: By participating, players explicitly acknowledge and agree that natural variations in table speeds, slow play by table mates, and individual player rotations are an inherent part of open play. These factors may naturally result in some players completing more rounds or games than others during a session. The Sponsor will not alter card requirements, compensate, or provide alternative squares due to table pacing, seat adjustments, or dealer rotations.

6. Pacing & Prizing Structure

Prizes are structured to reward both peak performance speed and completion volume. Because the card features a weekly calendar-restricted column (Column B), no full card Blackouts can be physically completed or submitted before Tuesday, September 22, 2026.

Grand Prizes (Race to Blackout)

The first three players to bring a 100% completed, fully initialed Blackout card (all 25 squares) to the front desk will win the top tier rewards:

  • 🥇 1st to Blackout: One Month of Free Membership (October 2026) at Bird Bam (which includes Unlimited Free Open Play and other perks). Membership will renew at $135/month unless winner notifies the Club in writing at least 7 prior to the end of October 2026.
  • 🥈 2nd to Blackout: 6 Free Open Play passes ($100 value) to use in October 2026.
  • 🥉 3rd to Blackout: 4 Free Open Play passes ($80 value) to use in October 2026.
  • 🥉 4th to Blackout: $50 Bird Bam Mahjong Club Shop Gift Certificate.
  • 🥉 5th to Blackout: One Free Ticket to 80’s Night on October 10, 2026 ($35 value).

Additional Blackout & Line Rewards

  • 🏅 All Other Blackouts: Every other player who successfully archives a full card Blackout before the end-of-month deadline will receive two (2) free Open Play session passes ($40 value) for use in October 2026.
  • 🀄 Any Single Line (No Blackout): Any participant who completes a single straight line of 5 squares (Horizontal, Vertical, or Diagonal) but does not achieve a full Blackout will receive one (1) free Open Play session pass ($20 value) valid for October 2026. Limit one single-line reward per player.

Member Substitutions

In the event that a Bird Bam monthly member wins an Open Play Grand Prize package, they may substitute the equivalent value as a shop gift certificate. If a member wins an Additional Blackout or Single Line Reward, those Open Play passes will be converted to Guest Passes.

7. Winner Selection & Fallback Safeguards

  • The Speed Race: For the top 3 spots, winners are determined strictly by the chronological timestamp of when their completed card is verified by front desk staff starting September 22.
  • The Fallback Clause: In the event that no participant achieves a full 25-square Blackout by the final deadline of 1:00 PM PT on September 30, 2026, the full Blackout tier will close. The top 5 grand prizes will instead be awarded to the five players who have accumulated the highest total volume of individual verified squares on their card.
  • Tie-Breaker Rule: If a tie occurs for total squares at the end of the month, the winner is the player who checked in earliest for their final play session on PlaybyPoint. Random drawings or games of chance will not be used.

8. General Conditions

  • This promotion is a pure contest of skill and achievement. No alternative method of entry (AMOE) is provided.
  • Subject to all applicable federal, state, and local laws and regulations.
  • Sponsor reserves the right to cancel, suspend, or modify the tournament format if fraud, technical failures, or structural issues compromise the integrity of the contest.

9. Privacy

Information collected from tracking cards and PlaybyPoint accounts will be used solely for the management of this contest and official club updates. Player data will never be sold or shared with third parties.

10. Liability Release

By participating, entrants agree to release, discharge, and hold harmless Bird Bam Mahjong Club, its owners, and staff from any and all injuries, liability, losses, or damages of any kind resulting from participation in this promotion or the acceptance/use of any prize.

11. Winners List

The complete winner list will be posted online no later than October 5, 2026.
