Questions

Frequently asked questions

Do I need any experience?

No. Learn Mahjong 1 starts from scratch: the tiles, the card, and how to build a hand. Most of our students have never played before.

What should I bring?

Just yourself. All equipment is provided, including tiles, racks, mats, and cards.

Can I come by myself?

Yes. Book solo, with a friend, or as a group. We'll group you with other students when needed.

I've played before. Where should I start?

If you already know the basics, you can start with Learn Mahjong 2: Guided Play Session. Most people start with Learn Mahjong 1.

How long are the classes?

Each class is 3 hours. The Learn to Play Package includes both classes plus a free 3-hour open play session, for 9 hours of mahjong.

What is American Mahjong?

American Mahjong is the most popular style of mahjong in the U.S. It's played with 152 tiles, including jokers and flowers, and a card of winning hands that the National Mah Jongg League publishes each year.

Do you teach other styles?

Not yet, but they're coming. Join the interest list for Chinese / Hong Kong, Riichi (Japanese), or Filipino mahjong, and we'll let you know when dates open.

Can I book a private class for my group?

Yes. Private and group classes are $75 per person, the same as our regular classes. Call or text (818) 564-7289 to schedule.

Can I give a class as a gift?

Yes. Lesson gift certificates are available online and delivered instantly by email. You can also call, text, or ask at the front desk.

Can I bring food or drinks?

Yes. You're welcome to bring your own, and drinks and packaged snacks are also available for purchase.

Where are you located?

Bird Bam Mahjong Club is at 17200 Ventura Blvd, Suite 115, on the lower level of the Encino Town Center. Free parking is available on all sides of the center, plus underground parking.
