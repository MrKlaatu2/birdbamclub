Mahjong Marathon – Official Rules

1. Sponsor

Mahjong Marathon (“Promotion”) is sponsored by Bird Bam Mahjong Club, located in the Encino Town Center, 17200 Ventura Blvd, Ste 115, Encino, CA 91316 (“Sponsor”).

2. Promotion Period

The Promotion runs from Sunday, August 2, 2026 through Sunday, August 30, 2026 (the “Promotion Period”). All tracking entries must be logged and verified during this period to count toward leaderboard standings.

3. Eligibility

  • Open to individuals 18+ who are legal U.S. residents, excluding employees/household members of the Sponsor.
  • Void where prohibited. Participation constitutes acceptance of official rules and finality of Sponsor decisions.

4. PlaybyPoint Registration

  • All entrants must have or create a free Bird Bam Mahjong Club account in PlaybyPoint, using this link: https://birdbammahjongclub.playbypoint.com/users/sign_up 
  • Information on score submissions must match the PlaybyPoint profile to avoid disqualification.

5. Participation & Score Tracking

  • Earn one point per verified "Mahjong" win during officially scheduled August American Mahjong Open Play sessions. Complete a mahjong slip by clearly printing your full name, email address, and phone number on the slip and drop it into the Mahjong Marathon box before leaving.
  • The Grand Opening Open Play scheduled on Saturday, August 15, 2026 is excluded from the Promotion.
  • Deadline: All entries must be finalized by 4:00 PM PT on Aug 30, 2026.

6. Leaderboard Conditions

  • Only complete, legible, and witnessed slips count.
  • Leaderboards will be displayed in-club and online.
  • Tampering or misconduct results in disqualification.

7. Prizes

Top 5 players, as of August 30, 2026, receive prizes:

  • 1st: Five (5) free Open Play passes (valid for scheduled Open Play sessions).
  • 2nd: $50 gift certificate to spend in the Bird Bam Mahjong Club shop.
  • 3rd: Set of 4 mahjong racks (choice from shop inventory).
  • 4th: Set of matching velvet rack and mat bags.
  • 5th: One pair of Engraved Metal Dice (choice from shop inventory.
  • Prizes are non-transferable, non-substitutable.

Prizes are non-transferable and non-substitutable. Tie-Breaker Rule: In the event of a tie in point totals for any leaderboard position, a blind random draw will be conducted by the Sponsor to determine the final ranking order for that position.

8. Winner Selection & Notification

  • Winners are determined strictly based on validated points from completed, witnessed slips.
  • In the event of a tie-breaker draw, the draw will be executed immediately following the 4:00 PM PT deadline on August 30, 2026.
  • The top 5 finalized winners will be notified via email, phone, or text.
  • Physical prizes require pickup at Bird Bam Mahjong Club within 30 days of notification.

9. General Conditions

  • The Promotion is subject to all applicable federal, state, and local laws and regulations.
  • Sponsor reserves the right to cancel, modify, or suspend the Promotion if fraud, technical failures, or any other factor beyond Sponsor’s reasonable control impairs the integrity or proper functioning of the Promotion, as determined by Sponsor in its sole discretion.
  • Sponsor reserves the right, in its sole discretion, to disqualify any individual it finds to be tampering with the entry process, violating these Official Rules, or acting in an unsportsmanlike or disruptive manner.

10. Privacy

Information collected via entry slips and PlaybyPoint may be used to administer this Promotion and to communicate with entrants about Bird Bam Mahjong Club events, promotions, and services, consistent with Sponsor’s general privacy practices and PlaybyPoint’s terms. Participants may unsubscribe from marketing communications at any time via the methods provided in those communications. Bird Bam Mahjong Club does not sell entrant information to third parties.

11. Liability Release

By participating, entrants agree to release and hold harmless Sponsor, Bird Bam Mahjong Club, and their respective owners, employees, and agents from and against any claim or cause of action arising out of participation in the Promotion or receipt or use of any prize, except where prohibited by law.
